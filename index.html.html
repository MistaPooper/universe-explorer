Galaxy

Model

Stars follow v(ρ) = v₀ρ/√(ρ²+1 kpc²). Spiral arms are a rigidly rotating density wave (Lin & Shu 1964), so stars drift through them. Positions are a model, not a star catalogue; values without a citation are illustrative.

Pulsar
Derived

Rotating-lighthouse model: cos γ = cos α cos ζ + sin α sin ζ cos φ; a pulse arrives when γ ≲ ρ (Radhakrishnan & Cooke 1969). Rotation is slowed for visibility; I = 10⁴⁵ g cm². The P−1/2 beam scaling follows from the polar-cap size; the 5.4° coefficient is an illustrative value between empirical cone fits.
